# Anthony Kosten

**Anthony Cornelis Kosten** (born 24 July 1958 in London, England), often published as **Tony Kosten**, is an English-French chess Grandmaster, author, opening theoretician, and coach.

## Career Overview
Kosten was among the prominent wave of English players who advanced through the international ranks during the English chess boom, securing the International Master (IM) title in 1984 and the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1990. He relocated to France in 1989, later representing the French Chess Federation internationally from 2002 until transferring back to the English federation in 2023. Throughout his playing career, Kosten achieved consistent success across major European opens and club leagues, reaching a peak FIDE rating of 2551 in July 2002. Beyond competitive play, he established himself as a distinguished opening specialist, serving as coach and captain for the French national team and leading the opening theory website *ChessPublishing.com*.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **Standout Tournament Victories:** Scored a historic performance at the 1st Geneva International Open (1986), taking clear first place with 8.5/9 after winning his first eight consecutive games against a 170-player field that included several leading grandmasters. He also won or shared first at the Cappelle-la-Grande Open (1987), Andorra Open (1985), Budapest (1984), and back-to-back Hastings Challengers Opens (1987–88 and 1989–90).
* **International Team Representation:** Represented England at the 1990 Reykjavik Visa Chess Summit, helping the national squad claim the team **silver medal**.
* **European League Titles:** Won the British Four Nations Chess League (4NCL) multiple times—with Slough (1996, 1999, 2000) and Guildford (2004, 2007, 2008)—and secured two French Team Championship titles with Monaco (2001, 2002). He also played on top board for TSV Schott Mainz in the German Bundesliga for over a decade.
* **National Championship Podiums:** Finished third at the 1982 British Chess Championship in Torquay.

## Writing, Theory, and Coaching
Kosten is widely recognized for his theoretical contributions to opening preparation and chess instruction:
* **ChessPublishing.com:** Founder and long-time chief editor of *ChessPublishing.com*, a pioneering subscription-based portal providing monthly theoretical coverage, analysis, and grandmaster insight across modern opening systems.
* **National Captaincy & Coaching:** Appointed captain and coach of the French national team following his move to France, mentoring players across multiple major international championships.
* **Authorship & Theoretical Works:** Authored numerous opening monographs and instruction books, with a notable specialty in flank systems and fighting repertoires, including:
  * *Winning Endgames* (1987)
  * *Winning with the Philidor* (1992)
  * *New Ideas in the Nimzo-Indian Defence* (1994)
  * *101 Tips to Improve Your Chess* (1996)
  * *The Dynamic English* (1999) – a highly regarded repertoire text on 1.c4 with 2.g3
  * *Easy Guide to the Najdorf* (1999)
  * *The Latvian Gambit Lives!* (2001)
  * *Dangerous Weapons: Flank Openings* (2008, co-author)
