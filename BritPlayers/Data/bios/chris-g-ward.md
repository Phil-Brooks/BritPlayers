# Chris Ward

**Christopher Geoffrey Ward** (born 26 March 1968) is an English chess Grandmaster, 1996 British Chess Champion, leading authority on the Sicilian Dragon, and prolific author and coach.

## Career Overview
Ward grew up in North West Kent, discovering chess at his village school club in Vigo. He steadily rose through the junior and national ranks, achieving the International Master (IM) title in 1989. In 1996, Ward scored the triumph of his career by winning the British Chess Championship in Nottingham, earning his final Grandmaster (GM) norm in the process to secure the GM title. Over his competitive career, he attained a peak FIDE rating of 2531 in July 2003. Celebrated for his uncompromising, attacking style, Ward is widely considered one of the world's most dedicated and influential exponents of the Sicilian Dragon. He has also established a prominent career as a senior coach, chess entertainer, and writer.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **British Chess Championship (1996):** Crowned British Champion in Nottingham, producing a memorable tournament run that sealed his Grandmaster title.
* **4NCL & Team Competition:** Longtime stalwart in the Four Nations Chess League (4NCL) and the German Bundesliga, scoring victories against top international grandmasters.
* **Open Tournament Honours:** Won or shared first place at numerous events across the British circuit, alongside frequent, high-scoring simultaneous exhibitions across clubs in the UK.

## The Sicilian Dragon, Authorship, and Coaching
Ward is known for his witty, approachable writing and coaching style, making complex tactical themes accessible to club players:
* **The "Dragon" Guru:** Regarded as the foremost modern British champion and author on the Sicilian Dragon (`...g6`), presenting monthly shows (*The Monthly Dragon*) and DVDs for ChessBase:
  * *Winning with the Sicilian Dragon* (1994)
  * *Winning with the Sicilian Dragon 2* (2001)
  * *Starting Out: The Sicilian Dragon* (2008)
* **Prolific Authorship:** Authored numerous opening treatises, endgame manuals, and puzzle books for Batsford and Everyman Chess, including:
  * *The Genius of Paul Morphy* (1997)
  * *The Queen's Gambit Accepted* (1999)
  * *Improve Your Opening Play* (2000)
  * *Starting Out: The Nimzo-Indian* (2002)
  * *Starting Out: Rook Endgames* (2004)
  * *The Controversial Sämisch King's Indian* (2004)
  * *It's Your Move: Tough Puzzles* (2004)
  * *Starting Out: Chess Tactics and Checkmates* (2006)
* **Youth Coaching & Culture:** Served as lead coach for the Kent Junior Chess Association (KJCA) and coached British junior squads at European and World Youth Championships. Outside chess, he is an enthusiastic salsa dancer.
