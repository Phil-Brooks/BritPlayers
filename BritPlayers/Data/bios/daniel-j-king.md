# Daniel King

**Daniel John King** (born 28 August 1963 in Beckenham, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, writer, coach, journalist, and broadcaster.

## Career Overview
King emerged during the height of the English chess boom in the 1970s and 1980s, earning the International Master (IM) title in 1982 and the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1989. Over a competitive career spanning more than two decades, he played professionally at top levels across Europe, notably in the German Bundesliga and the British 4NCL. He reached a peak FIDE rating of 2560 in July 1990, ranking among the world's top 100 players. As his career progressed, King successfully transitioned into media, establishing himself as one of the game's foremost international commentators, analysts, and chess educators.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **International Team Representation:** Represented England at the 1989 European Team Chess Championship in Haifa and at the 1990 Reykjavik Visa Chess Summit, where the English squad famously defeated the Soviet national team to claim the team **silver medal**.
* **Tournament Victories:** Shared first place with Boris Gelfand at the Sydney Open (1988) and with Ian Rogers at the Geneva Young Masters (1990). In 1996, he won the prestigious Bunratty Masters in Ireland.
* **Notable Podiums:** Recorded strong finishes at several major events, including second place in London (1989, behind Bent Larsen), joint second at Dortmund (1988), and tied fourth at both the Bern International (1987) and the British Chess Championship (1987).

## Broadcasting, Writing, and Media
King is widely celebrated for his clear, engaging presentation style, bringing grandmaster-level insight to broader audiences across print, television, and digital platforms:
* **Television & Commentary:** Rose to mainstream prominence as a lead studio commentator for Channel 4's coverage of the 1993 World Chess Championship match between Garry Kasparov and Nigel Short. He went on to cover landmark spectacles such as the 1997 Kasparov vs. Deep Blue rematch and Kasparov vs. The World (1999).
* **Digital Coaching & ChessBase:** Hosts the widely followed YouTube channel *PowerPlayChess* and produced the acclaimed *Power Play* instructional DVD and video series for ChessBase, alongside coaching many of the United Kingdom's top junior talents.
* **Journalism & Authorship:** Contributed regular chess columns to national newspapers such as *The Guardian* and long-running interactive features like "How Good Is Your Chess?" for *CHESS Magazine*. He is the author of more than 15 books, including:
  * *Kasparov v Deeper Blue: The Ultimate Man v Machine Challenge* (1997)
  * *How Good Is Your Chess?* (2003)
  * *Sultan Khan: The Indian Servant Who Became Chess Champion of the British Empire* (2020) – winner of the English Chess Federation Book of the Year award
