# David Norwood

**David Robert Norwood** (born 3 October 1968 in Farnworth, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, writer, entrepreneur, and venture capitalist who has represented both England and Andorra.

## Career Overview
Norwood rose rapidly through the English junior ranks during the 1980s chess boom, earning the International Master (IM) title in 1985 and the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1989. Known on the board for a dynamic, unorthodox, and uncompromising tactical style—frequently playing the Modern Defence and King's Indian Attack—he reached a peak FIDE rating of 2545 in July 1994. Norwood combined his competitive rise with higher education, graduating with a degree in history from Keble College, Oxford in 1988. He later scaled back over-the-board play to build a prominent career in finance and science commercialisation, while continuing to contribute significantly to chess as a national team captain, author, and benefactor.

## Chess Career and International Roles
* **International Representation:** Competed for England during his peak professional years before transferring his FIDE federation registration to Andorra in 2011, representing Andorra in international competition.
* **Team Leadership & Sponsorship:** Regularly served as a manager, sponsor, and captain of the English national squad at premier international team events, including the Chess Olympiad.
* **Junior Development Benefactor:** In 2001, he made a major financial donation to the British Chess Federation to support junior training, coaching, and talent development.
* **Signature Games:** Well known for his sharp miniature attacking victories, notably his win as White against Sean Marsh at Walsall in 1992.

## Business and Technology Venturing
Norwood established a notable second career in City investment banking, university spin-outs, and tech commercialisation:
* **Banking:** Joined the investment bank Bankers Trust in 1991 after completing his studies.
* **IP Group plc:** Founded IP Group, a pioneer in intellectual property commercialisation, which initially funded spin-out ventures from the University of Oxford’s Department of Chemistry in exchange for revenue-sharing and equity partnerships.
* **Oxford Sciences Innovation (OSI):** Co-founded and served as CEO (2015–2019) of Oxford Sciences Innovation, a £1.2 billion venture fund dedicated to translating Oxford University's scientific and deep-tech research into global companies.
* **Philanthropy at Keble College:** In 2017, he donated £1.9 million to Keble College, Oxford to support the establishment of a future innovation and research hub.

## Journalism and Authorship
In addition to writing regular chess columns and puzzle features for *The Daily Telegraph*, Norwood has authored several popular instructional and opening manuals:
* *King's Indian Attack* (1991)
* *Winning with the Modern* (1994)
* *Steve Davis Plays Chess* (co-authored with six-time snooker world champion Steve Davis)
* *The Modern Benoni* (1995)
* *Chess Puzzles* (1995)
