# Glenn Flear

**Glenn Curtis Flear** (born 12 February 1959 in Leicester, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, author, and coach, best known for creating one of the biggest upsets in international tournament history and for his authoritative literature on endgame and opening theory.

## Career Overview
Flear emerged during the 1970s and 1980s as part of the celebrated "English Chess Explosion." He attained the International Master (IM) title in 1983 and earned his Grandmaster (GM) title in 1987. His career breakthrough occurred at the 1986 GLC Chess Challenge in London: drafted into the Category 14 event as a last-minute replacement and the lone non-GM, Flear stunned the chess world by winning the tournament outright ahead of elite competitors such as Nigel Short, John Nunn, Boris Spassky, Zoltan Ribli, Lev Polugaevsky, Lajos Portisch, and Bent Larsen. In the middle of this historic victory, he took a scheduled rest day to marry French Woman International Master and five-time French Champion Christine Leroy. The couple later relocated to Montpellier, France.

## International Competition
* **Chess Olympiads and Team Events:** Represented England at the 1986 Dubai Chess Olympiad as a member of the powerhouse English squad that earned the team **silver medal**, finishing just behind the Soviet Union. He also represented his country at the inaugural 1985 World Team Championship in Lucerne (winning a team **bronze medal**) and the 2003 European Team Chess Championship in Plovdiv. In later years, he has represented England in senior international team events, winning multiple European Senior Team Championship titles.
* **World Championship Cycle:** Finished runner-up at the 1986 Bath Zonal tournament to qualify for the 1987 World Championship Interzonal in Szirák, Hungary.
* **Tournament Successes:** Alongside his landmark 1986 London victory, Flear has won or shared first place at numerous international events across Europe, including Brussels (1987), Palma de Mallorca (1991), Djerba (1998), Créon (1998, 2001), Montpellier (2001), and the Hastings International Chess Congress (2001/02).

## Writing and Coaching
Flear is widely respected as an author and theoretician, having published numerous books primarily focused on endgame strategy and comprehensive opening repertoires. His 2007 treatise *Practical Endgame Play – Beyond the Basics* is regarded as a modern classic on complex, multi-piece endgames ("Not Quite Endgames"). In addition to writing, he has coached several top youth prospects and authored long-running instructional columns for chess periodicals and training websites.

His most acclaimed books include:
* *Practical Endgame Play – Beyond the Basics* (2007)
* *Improve Your Endgame Play* (2000)
* *Mastering the Endgame* (2001)
* *Test Your Endgame Thinking* (2002)
* *Starting Out: Pawn Endgames* (2004)
