# James Howell

**James Clifford Howell** (born 17 May 1967 in Harrogate, England) is an English chess Grandmaster and author.

## Career Overview
Howell was recognized from an early age as one of England’s brightest junior prodigies. At just seven years old, he drew national attention by mounting a fierce challenge against Hungarian Grandmaster Lajos Portisch in a simultaneous exhibition—earning him a profile in the *Yorkshire Post* dubbed "The Wizard of Harrogate". Developing through the Harrogate Chess Club and English junior squad, he earned the International Master (IM) title in 1985 at age 18 and captained the British junior squad. While studying modern languages at Jesus College, Oxford, he starred on top boards in the annual Oxford–Cambridge Varsity matches. Howell achieved the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1995 and reached a peak FIDE rating of 2525 in July of that year. Having attained the highest title in chess, he retired from professional tournament play in 1996 to pursue a career in finance and accountancy.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **Junior World Championship Scalp:** At the 1985 World Junior Championship (U20) in Sharjah, Howell defeated future World Champion Viswanathan Anand in an 82-move battle.
* **National Championship Contender:** Regularly competed at the sharp end of the British Chess Championship throughout the late 1980s and early 1990s, finishing tied for second place at the 1994 British Championship.
* **Varsity Honors:** Led Oxford University across several Varsity matches against Cambridge in the mid-to-late 1980s, repeatedly winning the Lloyds Bank Trophy for the best individual game.
* **Grandmaster Title (1995):** Secured his three norms in elite European open and league competitions, officially becoming one of the select group of English grandmasters to emerge from the 1980s–90s generation.

## Authorship and Later Career
Despite his relatively brief period as a full-time professional grandmaster, Howell made an enduring contribution to instructional chess literature:
* **Classic Endgame Manual:** Authored ***Essential Chess Endings: The Tournament Player's Guide*** (Batsford, 1997; reprinted 2003). 
  * Unlike dense reference manuals such as *Basic Chess Endings*, Howell's book broke new ground by curating practical positions that arise most frequently in competitive over-the-board play.
  * It remains widely recommended by coaches and grandmasters for its concise, principle-based instruction and practical exercises.
* **Life Beyond Competitive Play:** After achieving his grandmaster title and publishing his endgame treatise, Howell stepped away from competitive circuit play to work in business and finance, remaining an honorary life member of Harrogate Chess Club.
