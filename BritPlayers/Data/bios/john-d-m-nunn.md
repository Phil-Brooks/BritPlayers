# John Nunn

**Dr John Denis Martin Nunn** (born 25 April 1955 in London, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, mathematician, three-time World Problem Solving Champion, and celebrated chess author and publisher.

## Career Overview
A mathematical and chess prodigy, Nunn entered Oriel College, Oxford, at age 15—becoming Oxford's youngest undergraduate in over 400 years. He earned his doctorate (D.Phil.) in algebraic topology in 1978 and lectured mathematics at Oxford until 1981, when he turned fully professional in chess. In over-the-board play, he won the European Junior Championship in 1975, earned the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1978, and captured the British Chess Championship in 1980. During the 1980s he established himself among the world elite, reaching a peak ranking of World No. 9.

## International Competition
* **Chess Olympiads:** Represented England at every Chess Olympiad between 1976 and 1994 during the golden era of British chess. He helped England secure team **silver medals** in 1984, 1986, and 1988, as well as a team **bronze medal** in 1990. At the 1984 Thessaloniki Olympiad, he won two individual **gold medals**—one on board two and another for the highest overall performance rating of the entire event.
* **Tournament Successes:** Won or shared first place at numerous premier international tournaments, including three victories at Wijk aan Zee (1982, 1990, 1991), Biel (1982, 1983), and Hastings (1993/94). In the inaugural 1988–89 GMA World Cup against the world's best, he finished sixth overall.
* **Problem Solving & Senior Championships:** One of the few players to hold Grandmaster titles in both competitive play and problem solving, Nunn won the **World Chess Solving Championship** in 2004, 2007, and 2010. He later captured back-to-back titles at the **World Senior Championship (Over 65)** in 2022 and 2023.

## Writing and Publishing
Renowned for his analytical precision, Nunn is widely considered one of the finest chess writers of modern times and a multiple winner of the British Chess Federation Book of the Year award. In 1997, he co-founded **Gambit Publications** alongside Murray Chandler and Graham Burgess, serving as its chess director.

His most acclaimed books include:
* *Secrets of Practical Chess* (1998)
* *Understanding Chess Move by Move* (2001)
* *John Nunn's Chess Course* (2014)
* *Solving in Style* (1985)
