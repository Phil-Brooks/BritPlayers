# John Emms

**John Michael Emms** (born 14 March 1967) is an English chess Grandmaster, prolific author, senior coach, and long-standing commissioning editor for Everyman Chess.

## Career Overview
Emms learned chess at an early age and developed into one of England's steadiest grandmasters during the 1990s. He earned the International Master (IM) title in 1990 and achieved the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1995. A formidable competitor across European leagues and open tournaments, Emms reached a peak FIDE rating of 2586 in July 1999, placing him among the world's top players. Alongside competitive play, he built an influential career as one of the most widely read and respected instructional authors and chess publishing figures in the world, notably serving as the commissioning editor of Everyman Chess.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **British Championship:** Tied for first place at the 1997 British Chess Championship, narrowly missing out on the title in the subsequent playoff.
* **International Open Victories:** Scored back-to-back shared first-place finishes at the prestigious Politiken Cup in Denmark in both 1992 and 1993.
* **Olympiad & Team Representation:** Represented England at the 34th Chess Olympiad in Istanbul (2000) and the 35th Chess Olympiad in Bled (2002). He served as the captain of the English national team at the 2002 Olympiad and coached the English women's squad at the 2004 Calvià Olympiad.
* **Senior Team Honors:** Represented England at senior level, capturing team and individual board gold at the European Senior Team Championship (50+).

## Publishing, Coaching, and Authorship
Emms has shaped modern chess literature as both an author and editorial director:
* **Editorial Leadership:** As the general commissioning editor for Everyman Chess (formerly Cadogan / Popular Chess), Emms developed and supervised landmark instructional lines, including the widely acclaimed *Starting Out* and *Move by Move* series.
* **Elite Coaching:** An experienced trainer and second who has worked with top-tier grandmasters, including World Championship finalist Michael Adams.
* **Major Publications:** Authored and co-authored dozens of respected opening treatises, puzzle collections, and endgame manuals, including:
  * *Easy Guide to the Ruy Lopez* (1999)
  * *Nunn's Chess Openings* (1999, with John Nunn, Joe Gallagher, and Graham Burgess)
  * *The Survival Guide to Rook Endings* (1999)
  * *The Ultimate Chess Puzzle Book* (2000)
  * *Simple Chess* (2001)
  * *Starting Out: The Sicilian* (2002)
  * *The Mammoth Book of the World's Greatest Chess Games* (co-authored with Graham Burgess and John Nunn)
  * *The Nimzo-Indian: Move by Move* (2011)
  * *The Sicilian Taimanov: Move by Move* (2012)
