# Joseph Gallagher

**Joseph Gerald Gallagher** (born 4 May 1964 in London, England) is an English-Swiss chess Grandmaster and author.

## Career Overview
Gallagher was raised in London as the eldest of six siblings in an active chess-playing family of Irish descent. He emerged as a formidable attacking player on the competitive European open circuit during the 1980s, earning the International Master (IM) title in 1987 and the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1990. After marrying, he settled in Neuchâtel, Switzerland, and acquired Swiss nationality, switching federation representation from England to Switzerland in the late 1990s. Gallagher reached a peak FIDE rating of 2558 in January 2000. Known for his sharp, tactical style and dedication to uncompromising systems like the King's Indian Defence, he achieved the rare feat of winning the national championship of both Britain and Switzerland.

## National Championships and International Representation
* **Dual National Champion:** Gallagher achieved the rare distinction of winning national titles across two federations:
  * **British Chess Championship:** Won in 2001.
  * **Swiss Chess Championship:** Won a total of seven titles across four different decades (1997, 1998, 2004, 2005, 2007, 2012, and 2021).
* **International Team Representation:** Served as a cornerstone of the Swiss national team, participating in every Chess Olympiad from 1998 to 2014 (with a standout score of 6½/10 at the 2006 Turin Olympiad), ten European Team Chess Championships (1997–2015), and the World Team Chess Championship in 1997.
* **League and Club Success:** Played extensively in domestic European team leagues, notably capturing multiple Four Nations Chess League (4NCL) team titles in Britain with squads such as Beeson Gregory and Guildford A&DC.

## Authorship and Theoretical Contributions
Beyond competitive over-the-board play, Gallagher is widely celebrated as one of modern chess's most prominent opening authors, renowned for lucid explanations, aggressive repertoire choices, and thorough theoretical insights:
* **Opening Repertoire Authority:** Established himself as one of the world's foremost authorities on the King's Indian Defence with Black, as well as an advocate and leading expert on the King's Gambit with White.
* **Collaborative Reference Works:** Served as one of four co-authors of the landmark reference manual *Nunn's Chess Openings* (1999) alongside John Nunn, Graham Burgess, and John Emms, and co-wrote *Beating the Sicilian 3* (1995) with Nunn.
* **Selected Publications:** Authored numerous influential opening treatises, middlegame manuals, and game collections, including:
  * *Winning With the King's Gambit* (1992)
  * *The Trompovsky* (1998)
  * *101 Attacking Ideas in Chess* (2000)
  * *The Magic of Mikhail Tal* (2001)
  * *Starting Out: The King's Indian* (2002)
  * *Starting Out: The Pirc/Modern* (2003)
  * *Play the King's Indian* (2004)
