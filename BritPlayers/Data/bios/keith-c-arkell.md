# Keith Arkell

**Keith Charles Arkell** (born 8 January 1961 in Birmingham, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, English Chess Champion, Senior European Champion, author, and acclaimed endgame specialist.

## Career Overview
Arkell took up chess at the age of 13 and steadily climbed the ranks of British chess, earning the International Master (IM) title in 1985 and the Grandmaster (GM) title a decade later in 1995 after scoring norms at Ostend (1990), Parthenay (1993), and the French League (1995). He recorded a peak FIDE rating of 2545 in July 1996. A legendary and prolific figure on the open congress circuit, Arkell has won hundreds of open and weekend tournaments throughout the United Kingdom and internationally. Over his veteran career, he achieved world-class recognition in senior chess, winning the European Senior (50+) Championship, tying for first place in the World Senior (50+) Championship, and repeatedly earning English Chess Federation Player of the Year honors.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **National Championships:** Crowned English Chess Champion in 2008 after tying for first place at the British Chess Championship with Stuart Conquest (losing the overall title on a blitz playoff). He was also the British Rapidplay Champion in 1998 and won the British Online Championship in 2021.
* **Senior World & European Successes:** Won individual **gold** at the 2014 European Senior (50+) Championship in Porto, and earned the individual **silver medal** at the 2014 World Senior (50+) Championship in Katerini after tying for first with Zurab Sturua (8.5/11). In 2022, he took double team gold with England at the World Senior Team and European Senior Team Championships, taking board gold on both occasions.
* **Open Tournament Exploits:** Shared first prize (placing second on tiebreaks) in a massive 465-player field at the 2015 Vienna Open. Other career highlights include tying for second at the 2001 British Championship and the 2002/03 Hastings Premier, winning the 2002 Wroxham Masters, and scoring a notable victory over an 11-year-old Magnus Carlsen at Gausdal (2002). During a 2007–2008 tour of the United States, he recorded wins at the Marshall Chess Club, Saratoga Springs, and the Blackstone Open.

## Playing Style, Opening Contributions, and Authorship
Arkell is celebrated for his deep positional understanding, technical clarity, and exceptional endgame mastery—often drawing comparisons to Anatoly Karpov and Ulf Andersson:
* **The "Arkell–Khenkin" Variation:** Known as one of the world's leading experts on the Caro–Kann Defence. His innovative treatment of the Advance Variation with `1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.e5 c5!?` led *New In Chess* to christen the system the **"Arkell–Khenkin Line"**.
* **Publications:** Arkell has shared his unique chess journey and endgame expertise through two well-received books:
  * *Arkell's Odyssey* (2012) – An autobiographical chronicle of life as an active British chess professional.
  * *Arkell's Endings* (2020) – A practical manual breaking down the principles behind his technical endgame victories.
