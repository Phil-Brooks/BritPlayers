# Matthew Sadler

**Matthew David Sadler** (born 15 May 1974 in Chatham, Kent, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, two-time British Chess Champion, award-winning author, and leading analyst of artificial intelligence in chess.

## Career Overview
Sadler emerged as one of Britain's most gifted chess prodigies during the late 1980s, earning the International Master (IM) title in 1988 at the age of 14 and the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1993 at 19. During the late 1990s, he established himself among the international elite, reaching a peak world ranking of No. 16 and a peak FIDE rating of 2694. In the early 2000s, at the height of his career, Sadler stepped away from full-time professional play to pursue a career in information technology in the Netherlands. Returning to tournament chess as an amateur in 2010, he mounted an extraordinary competitive comeback, winning a series of international events and returning to the world top 100. Alongside his competitive career, Sadler is widely regarded as one of chess's foremost writers and analysts of modern chess-engine technology.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **British National Championships:** Won the British Chess Championship twice—first in 1995 at age 21, and again in 1997 (jointly with Michael Adams).
* **Olympiad Individual Gold:** Represented England at the 1996 Yerevan Chess Olympiad, scoring an exceptional 10.5/13 to secure the individual **gold medal** for best performance on Board 4. He also represented England at the 1998 Elista Olympiad and the 2018 Batumi Olympiad.
* **Historic European Team Gold:** Scored 7/9 on Board 4 at the 1997 European Team Chess Championship in Pula, turning in the best individual score of the squad to propel England to its first (and to date only) team gold medal in major international competition.
* **Comeback Victories (2010–2011):** After a long hiatus, returned in 2010 with a 7/7 sweep at Wageningen ahead of Jan Timman, followed by victories at the 2011 Barcelona Open (8.5/10) and the 2011 Oslo Chess International (8/9 with a 2849 performance rating).

## Authorship, Computer Chess, and Media
Sadler is a leading chronicler of the game and a multiple-time winner of major literature prizes:
* **Award-Winning Authorship:** Author of influential opening monographs and celebrated analytical studies:
  * *The Slav* (1997)
  * *The Semi-Slav* (1998)
  * *Tips for Young Players* (1999)
  * *Queen's Gambit Declined* (2000) – Winner of the British Chess Federation Book of the Year award
  * *Chess for Life* (2016, with Natasha Regan) – Winner of the ECF Book of the Year award
  * *Game Changer: AlphaZero’s Groundbreaking Chess Strategies and the Promise of AI* (2019, with Natasha Regan) – Winner of both the ECF Book of the Year and the inaugural FIDE Book of the Year award
  * *The Silicon Road to Chess Improvement* (2021)
  * *Re-Engineering the Chess Classics* (2023, with Steve Giddins)
* **Journalism & Digital Channels:** Served for years as the regular book reviewer for *New In Chess* magazine. He explores neural network engines (AlphaZero, Leela Chess Zero, Stockfish) and cutting-edge engine analysis through his platform and YouTube channel, *Silicon Road Chess*.
