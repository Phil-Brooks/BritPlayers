# Neil McDonald

**Neil McDonald** (born 21 January 1967 in Gravesend, Kent, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, FIDE Trainer, senior coach, and one of the chess world's most prolific instructional authors.

## Career Overview
McDonald emerged as one of England's brightest talents during the mid-1980s, achieving the International Master (IM) title in 1986 at the age of 19. Over the following decade, he established himself as a stalwart on the British circuit and European leagues, earning his Grandmaster (GM) title in 1996 and achieving a peak FIDE rating of 2500 in July 1996. A deeply respected teacher and mentor, McDonald has dedicated much of his career to coaching, serving as a key trainer for the English Chess Federation (ECF) and working with top junior talents as well as visually impaired players on the international stage. He is best known internationally for authoring nearly 40 instructional chess books renowned for their clarity and logical explanations.

## Competitive and Coaching Achievements
* **International Titles:** Awarded the International Master title in 1986 and attained the Grandmaster title in 1996. Qualified as an official FIDE Trainer in 2016.
* **National Youth Coaching:** Appointed Head Coach for the English Chess Federation squad at the 2013 World Schools Championship in Halkidiki, Greece, and has guided numerous English junior champions through national development pathways.
* **Service to Visually Impaired Chess:** For many years, McDonald has regularly assisted, coached, and escorted blind and partially sighted chess players representing England at international Braille Chess Association (IBCA) World Championship tournaments.
* **Opening Theory Contributions:** Authored regular monthly theoretical digests for *ChessPublishing.com* across nearly two decades, serving as their resident expert on the French Defence and `1.e4` repertoires.

## Authorship and Instructional Works
McDonald is recognized as one of the most accessible chess educators in print, with a focus on thought processes, strategic planning, and practical calculation:
* **Selected Notable Books:**
  * *Winning With the Kalashnikov* (1995)
  * *Mastering the French* (1997, with Andrew Harley)
  * *Concise Chess Endings* (2002)
  * *Chess: The Art of Logical Thinking* (2004)
  * *The Art of Planning in Chess: Move by Move* (2006)
  * *Chess Secrets: The Giants of Power Play* (2009)
  * *Starting Out: The Reti* (2010)
  * *Break the Rules! A Modern Look at Chess Strategy* (2012)
  * *Coach Yourself: A Complete Guide to Improving at Chess* (2019)
  * *Dynamic Defence* (2021)
