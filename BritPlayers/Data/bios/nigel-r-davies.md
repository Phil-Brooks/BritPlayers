# Nigel Davies

**Nigel Rodney Davies** (born 31 July 1960 in Southport, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, FIDE Senior Trainer, prolific author, and coach.

## Career Overview
Davies emerged as a leading English junior talent during the British chess boom of the late 1970s, winning the British Under-21 Championship in 1979. He earned the International Master (IM) title in 1982 and achieved the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1993. He reached a peak FIDE rating of 2530 in January 1995. Over the course of his international career, Davies represented England, spent a period registered with the Israeli Chess Federation (1991–1993), transferred to Wales in 2015 (where he became the country's highest-rated player), and subsequently returned to the English federation in 2022. In addition to over-the-board competition, Davies is widely known as one of the chess world's most prolific writers, instructional video presenters, and holistic chess trainers.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **National & Junior Titles:** Won the British Under-21 Boys Championship (1979) and captured the British Rapidplay Chess Championship in 1987. Later in his veteran career, he also secured the British Senior (50+) Championship title.
* **International Tournament Victories:** Scored notable international tournament victories, including first-place finishes at the Oslo Open (1988), the Gausdal International (1989), and the Regina Open (1992), alongside repeated victories at open events such as the Blackpool Open and Heart of Finland.
* **Team Representation:** Represented Wales on top board at the 1989 European Team Chess Championship (scoring 5.5/9) and participated in European team qualification events for England.

## Coaching, Authorship, and Philosophy
Davies is acclaimed for making sophisticated grandmaster strategies accessible to everyday club players through a vast library of instructional material:
* **Authorship:** Authored dozens of opening guides, practical strategy manuals, and instructional books for leading chess publishers, including Batsford and Everyman Chess. Selected notable works include:
  * *The Chess Player's Battle Manual* (1998)
  * *The Power Chess Program* (1998)
  * *The Grünfeld Defence* (2002)
  * *The Dynamic Reti* (2004)
  * *The Trompowsky* (2005)
  * *Starting Out: The Modern* (2008)
  * *Play the Catalan* (2009)
* **Digital Coaching:** Produced numerous multimedia instructional courses and opening DVDs for ChessBase's *FritzTrainer* series. He also founded the online learning platform *Tiger Chess* and was a key contributor to *The Chess Improver* educational blog.
* **Mind-Body Integration:** An avid practitioner and certified instructor of Tai Chi Chuan (Taijiquan), Davies is distinct in the chess community for integrating martial arts philosophies, breathing techniques, and posture control into chess psychology and competitive stamina.
