# Paul Littlewood

**Paul Edwin Littlewood** (born 25 January 1956) is an English International Master, former British Chess Champion, and prominent coach and chess author.

## Chess Background & Heritage
Littlewood belongs to one of Britain's most notable chess families. He is the son of John E. Littlewood and nephew of Norman Littlewood, both of whom were leading British masters and Olympiad competitors throughout the 1960s and 1970s.

## Career & Achievements
* **British Chess Championship (1981):** Littlewood achieved his greatest career tournament triumph at Morecambe in 1981, winning the British Championship with a commanding score of 9/11.
* **International Master Title:** Awarded the title of International Master (IM) by FIDE in 1980.
* **Olympiad Representation:** Selected for the English national team at the 25th Chess Olympiad in Lucerne (1982), playing on board four.
* **World Student & Youth Teams:** Represented England at the World Under-26 Team Championship in Mexico City (1978), securing individual and team honors.

## Coaching & Publications
Beyond active play, Littlewood dedicated much of his career to chess education, coaching regional and national junior squads. He has authored several popular training manuals, including:
* *Chess Tactics* (1984)
* *Chess: A Complete Introduction* (Teach Yourself series)
