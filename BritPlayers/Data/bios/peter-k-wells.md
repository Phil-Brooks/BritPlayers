# Peter K Wells

**Peter Kenneth Wells** (born 17 April 1965 in Portsmouth, England) is an English chess Grandmaster and author. He earned the Grandmaster title in 1994 and won the British Rapidplay Chess Championship three times.

## Career Overview

Wells became one of England's established tournament players during the 1980s and 1990s. His FIDE rating reached 2545 in July 1995. He has continued to compete internationally across several decades; the game collection on this site includes 2,823 of his games, dated from 1979 to 2026.

A notable part of his domestic record is his success in rapid chess. Wells won the British Rapidplay Championship in **2002, 2003, and 2007**, taking the national title in three separate years.

## International Competition

Wells has played in a wide range of British and international events. In August 2025, he faced ten-year-old Bodhana Sivanandan in the British Championship. Her victory was reported as making her the youngest girl to defeat a Grandmaster in a classical tournament game; the result also completed the norms for her Woman International Master title.

## Writing and Publications

Wells has written opening books and instructional works, both independently and with co-authors. His books include:

* *The Complete Semi-Slav* (1994)
* *Piece Power* (1994)
* *The Complete Richter-Rauzer* (1998, with Viacheslav Osnos)
* *The Scotch Game* (1998)
* *Winning with the Trompowsky* (2004)
* *Chess Explained: The Queen's Indian* (2006)
* *Grandmaster Secrets: The Caro-Kann* (2007)
* *Dangerous Weapons: Anti-Sicilians* (2009, with John Emms and Richard Palliser)
* *Chess Improvement: It's All in the Mindset* (2020, with Barry Hymer)
