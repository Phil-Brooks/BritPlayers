# Stuart Conquest

**Stuart C. Conquest** (born 1 March 1967 in Ilford, England) is an English chess Grandmaster, commentator, and tournament director.

## Career Overview
Conquest emerged as one of Britain's brightest young talents during the English chess boom, gaining international recognition in 1981 when he won the World Youth Chess Championship Under-16 title at age 14. He earned the International Master (IM) title in 1985 and was awarded the Grandmaster (GM) title in 1991. A mainstay of top-tier English and European league chess—including regular appearances in the British 4NCL and the German Bundesliga—Conquest achieved a peak FIDE rating of 2601 in October 2001, ranking among the world's top 100 players and solidifying his position as the English number four behind Michael Adams, Nigel Short, and Matthew Sadler. In later years, he transitioned seamlessly into elite tournament directorship and media commentary, becoming one of the most beloved and recognizable figures on the international tournament circuit.

## International and Tournament Successes
* **International Team Representation:** Represented England at four Chess Olympiads (Yerevan 1996, Bled 2002, Turin 2006, and Dresden 2008) and four European Team Chess Championships (1999, 2001, 2007, and 2009).
* **Major Championship Titles:**
  * **British Chess Champion (2008):** Captured the prestigious national title in Liverpool after tying for first with Keith Arkell and winning the rapidplay play-off match 1.5–0.5.
  * **British Rapidplay Champion (1997):** Won the national rapidplay crown.
  * **World Youth U-16 Champion (1981):** Claimed the global junior title in Embalse, Argentina.
* **International Tournament Victories:**
  * **Hastings Premier:** Shared first place at the historic event twice, in 1995/96 (with Alexander Khalifman and Bogdan Lalić) and 2000/01 (with Krishnan Sasikiran).
  * **Clichy (2001):** Won the Category 14 GM round-robin tournament in France.
  * **Open Victories:** First-place finishes at tournaments including the Capo d'Orso Open (Palau 2009), Fürth (1999), Olot (1994), and joint wins at Calvià (2004) and San Sebastián (2006).

## Commentary, Directorship, and Promotion
Conquest is renowned in the chess community for his charisma, wit, and deep organizational contributions:
* **Gibraltar International Chess Festival:** Served as Tournament Director from 2011 to 2020. Under his stewardship, the Tradewise Gibraltar Masters grew into widely recognized status as the strongest open chess tournament in the world, attracting reigning World Champions and elite grandmasters to the Caleta Hotel.
* **Hastings International Chess Congress:** Appointed Tournament Director and Chairman of the organizing committee for the world's oldest and most historic recurring chess congress, overseeing its preservation and major milestones.
* **Broadcasting & Commentary:** One of the most entertaining and perceptive live broadcast analysts in the sport, regularly anchoring live coverage for premier events alongside co-commentators such as Simon Williams, Tania Sachdev, and Jovanka Houska.
